# Research

These notes came with the kit. They were written between 2026-09-25 and 2026-09-29 while building Days of
Odd, and they cover YouTube's policies, Shorts strategy, free compute, free language models, free image
generation, voices, and open-source tools. Personal details have been removed. Most of it doesn't depend
on the niche.

Treat the numbers as a snapshot: re-check anything a decision rests on (quotas, prices, terms, and policy
change often), and date what you add. `tools-radar.md` is the weekly checklist for new tools and policy
changes (`PLAYBOOK.md`, Sundays).
