## Reversible pipeline change

The landscape card keeps the entire archival print, moves its lower edge consistently just above the caption area, and leaves a recognizably detailed copy of the source across the background. The [before and after contact sheet](../../reports/previews/history-card-before-after-contact.jpg) shows three actual source images as still plates without captions. A [seven-second renderer proof](../../reports/previews/history-card-motion-proof.mp4) uses the R101 portrait and wreck images, the same camera move, grade, font and word timings as ep025; it has no audio. The [portrait](../../reports/previews/r101-thomson-captioned-proof.jpg) and [wreck](../../reports/previews/r101-wreck-captioned-proof.jpg) captioned frames show legibility at 1080 × 1920. Commons lists the [Lord Thomson portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Christopher_Birdwood_Thomson,_1st_Baron_Thomson,_in_1926.jpg) as public domain, credited to Bassano Ltd. A synthetic image test checks lower-frame visibility and caption clearance.

The review prompt now supplies the chosen image's title, recorded date and actual-subject note beside each frame, and asks the reviewer to flag modern substitutes for historical events and unreadably small prints. The recorded date is archival metadata and needs human interpretation: an upload date can differ from an image's creation date.

The per-episode editorial lock keeps held drafts out of scheduling and preflight; the channel scheduling lock pauses **new** posts while production can continue. Both are files in the repo and can be removed after repair. Existing Buffer posts need separate remote action; a local lock alone does not stop them.
