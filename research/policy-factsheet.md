**1. YPP eligibility**
- Fan-funding tier (India eligible): 500 subs, 3 uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days ([13429240](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240)).
- Ads tier now: 1,000 subs plus 4,000 hours in 12 months (Shorts-feed hours excluded) or 10M Shorts views in 90 days ([72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)).
- **From Feb 1, 2027**, new applicants need 1,000 subs plus 8,000 hours or 20M Shorts views. Channels "already in YPP" aren't affected ([12843009](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009)). **Unverified:** whether joining the fan-funding tier first locks in the old ads thresholds.
- Also required: no active strikes, 2-Step Verification, advanced features, one AdSense account, and an eligible country (India is listed, [7101720](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7101720)). You must be 18+ or have a guardian run AdSense ([72857](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857)).
- Review takes "typically about 1 month". Appeal within 21 days or reapply after 30 ([72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)).

**2. Inauthentic content** ([1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392))
- Renamed from "repetitious" July 15, 2025, then split (July 2026, per [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/) only) into Generic/Repetitive, Unsatisfying/Off-putting, and AI Personas on Sensitive Topics.
- Not allowed: templated videos with minimal variation, slideshows with little narrative, "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates", AI "experts" giving health, finance, or legal advice, and readings of others' text.
- Allowed: "using AI to edit your video scripts or generate a unique background visual".
- Spam Policy example: "same background music and repetitive AI generated imagery… each video reading out an AI-generated script" ([2801973](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).
- **Not named:** TTS, stock footage, or faceless formats.

**3. AI disclosure** ("AI use" field, [14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491))
- Required for realistic AI: real people doing things they didn't, altered real events, or realistic invented scenes. "AI generated music" is also listed. Not required: non-realistic content, AI help with scripts or thumbnails, or cloning your own voice.
- **Unverified:** generic TTS narrators aren't addressed. AI illustrations need disclosure only if realistic.
- Since May 2026, undisclosed photorealistic AI is auto-labeled, and labels don't affect recommendations or monetization ([blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/)). Labels triggered by C2PA metadata can't be removed. Repeated non-disclosure risks removal or YPP suspension.

**4. Verification** ([9890437](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437))
- Phone verification unlocks videos over 15 minutes, custom thumbnails, live streaming, and copyright-claim appeals ([171664](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171664)).
- Advanced features (channel history, or ID/video verification) unlock clickable description links, pinned comments, A/B testing, and higher daily limits ([9891124](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9891124)).
- **Conflicts:** the feature table says Content ID appeals need advanced features and Related Video needs only phone verification. [171664](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171664) and [14075157](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157) say the opposite.

**5. Upload limits**
- No number is published. The 24-hour cap spans web, mobile, and API, and "may vary by country/region or channel history" ([10383400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10383400)).
- API uploads default to 100 per day per project ([quota](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost)). Uploads from unaudited API projects stay private ([videos.insert](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert)).

**6. Shorts**
- Up to 3 minutes ([15424877](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)).
- Revenue comes from a Shorts ads pool; creators keep 45%. **From Feb 2027**, earning needs 10M views in the prior 90 days ([12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)).
- Since March 31, 2025, every Short play or replay counts as a view ([API changelog](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history)). Since Aug 24, 2026, views count when playback starts in all formats, but earnings use engaged views ([12220281](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281)).

**7. Metadata**
- More than 60 hashtags means all are ignored. Up to three "most engaging" show by the title ([6390658](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658)).
- Tags "play a minimal role" ([146402](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402)).
- "Recording date and location" still exists ([57407](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407)). **Unverified:** any effect on discovery.

**8. Spam and fake engagement**
- Banned: sub4sub, rewarding viewers for engagement, bought views, and malicious clickbait. Asking for subscriptions is fine, and hired promoters' violations count against you ([3399767](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767), [2801973](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).
- A first violation gets a warning; 3 strikes in 90 days can mean termination ([2801973](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)). Monetization can be turned off and earnings withheld ([1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).
- Since Dec 2024, egregious clickbait is removed, first in India and initially without strikes ([Google India](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/strengthening-enforcement-against-egregious-clickbait-on-youtube/)).

**9. Growth tools**
- Test & Compare tries up to 3 titles or thumbnails. It needs advanced features and excludes Shorts ([13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)).
- Auto-dubbing has been "available to everyone" in 27 languages since Feb 2026 ([blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-auto-dubbing-expressive-speech/)).
- Posts have no subscriber minimum ([9890437](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437)).
- Hype is for YPP channels with 500–500K subs (India included), in a video's first 7 days, not Shorts ([15509925](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15509925)).
- Announced Sep 23, 2026: Ask Studio feedback and dynamic thumbnails; A/B tests of video cuts are "coming soon" ([blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-new-tools-power-creation-journey/)). **Unverified:** eligibility.

**10. Channel country**
- "Eligibility for the YouTube Partner Program is based on" this setting ([2976814](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2976814)).
- Tax residence comes from AdSense tax info. The India treaty rate is 15% of US earnings; without tax info by Dec 10, up to 24% of all earnings is withheld ([10391362](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10391362)).
- **Unverified:** an explicit must-match rule. The field is labeled "Country of residence", so set India.
