**Bottom line:** English space documentaries, owner-narrated (Section 6). Treat 100K in 100 days as a stretch: only 1.3% of channels ever reach 100K (Section 4).

## 2. Playbook

- **Lead with long-form.** Among 2025-born channels that reached 1K, the median was 44 uploads for long-form, 81 for Shorts-only, and 127 for mixed ([vidIQ](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vidiq_everyone-says-shorts-are-the-fastest-way-activity-7437546158547193859-5wyj)). On vidIQ's own channel, long-form converted viewers to subscribers ~4.7× better (single channel, unverified, [transcript](https://rosetta.to/u/vidiq/the-fastest-way-to-1k-subs-in-2026)). Shorts still win reach: 86.5% of Shorts channels vs 57.8% of long-form channels had a 1K-view video among their first ten ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/)).
- **Shorts as bridges:** tease that week's long video, then add the Related-video link and a pinned comment. Aviation Tech hit 1K in 31 same-topic uploads ([vidIQ data](https://www.skool.com/inbound-on-youtube/how-many-videos-does-1000-subscribers-actually-take)).
- **Cadence:** 2 long-form and 3–4 Shorts weekly. Channels posting 12+ times a month grew a median 0.91% a month, vs 0.39% at 4–7 posts ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-upload-frequency-study/)).
- **Length:** 9–14 minutes, since mid-rolls start at 8 ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006)), plus a monthly 30–40-minute compilation; Galloway says longer videos are winning in the TV era ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paddy-galloway-459b8913a_how-to-rapidly-grow-a-channel-in-the-new-activity-7500552478762029058-DnUM)).
- **Packaging:** Colin & Samir put 75–80% of effort into title and thumbnail ([summary](https://lobstercast.com/cast/colin-samir-dominate-youtube-2026/)). Of 500 breakout thumbnails, 89% had a face or high contrast and 28% had no text ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)); faceless, use one bold, high-contrast subject and few words. Cosmic Lens's hits use "The Terrifying Truth About ___" and "___ Is NOT What We Thought" ([videos](https://www.youtube.com/@CosmicLens-EN/videos)). A/B test in [Test & Compare](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714).
- **Series:** named, repeatable formats; vary each episode's structure so nothing looks templated.
- **First 48 hours:** reply to comments, post a Community teaser, add end screens and playlists, and check Shorts' "viewed vs swiped away" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU)). Once in YPP, fans can [Hype](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15334500) new uploads.
- **Promotion:** [Collaborations](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/); watermark-free native uploads to Reels, TikTok, and Facebook ([Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality)); on Reddit, follow subreddit rules, keep self-promotion to ~10% ([Reddit](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28012014962580-How-do-I-keep-spam-out-of-my-community)), and [never ask for votes](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/205926439-Reddiquette).
- **Never:** sub4sub, bought engagement, rewarded engagement, comment spam, or spammy polls ([policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767), [spam](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).
- **Voice:** record it yourself. YouTube says viewers place "a premium on substantial human effort" ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/12/15/youtube-2025-culture-and-trends/)), and faceless AI channels are reportedly being demonetized ([THR](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/digital/faceless-creators-youtube-ai-damage-1236617586/)).

## 4. Benchmarks

- Of 61.2M channels with 1+ subscriber, 40.6% reached 1K, 7.9% 10K, and 1.3% 100K ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-subscriber-benchmarks-2026/)). 100K in 100 days is unmeasured and far rarer.
- Fewer than 1 in 100 channels with 1K+ subscribers grow 10%+ in a month ([CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/research/state-of-the-creator-economy/)); a 12-channel survey averaged 51 months to 100K ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewTubers/comments/1btkfbl/compiled_a_database_of_how_long_it_took_channels/)).
- Fastest faceless: Sleepless Historian ~500K in ~2 months (unverified, [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ahtisham-nawaz-715074265_youtubeautomation-facelessniches-history-activity-7344054531439169537-GNXc)); [Helix²](https://www.youtube.com/@H3lixSquar3d/about) 230K from 53 Shorts (timing unverified); [Cosmic Lens](https://www.youtube.com/@CosmicLens-EN/about) ~100K in 11 months.

## 5. English vs Hindi

- **RPM:** US $3–11 vs India $0.50–2; Hindi entertainment $0.30–0.80 ([aggregator, directional](https://ytmoneycalculator.com/blog/average-youtube-rpm/)). 1M long-form views pay $3K–15K ([vidIQ](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7471047836873158656)), roughly $0.5K–2K at India's rates (my calculation).
- **Growth:** 15,000+ Indian channels have passed 1M subscribers ([YouTube](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/neal-mohan-creator-economy-waves-2025/)), and India nearly matches the US in 100K+ channels (103.5K vs 109.6K, [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/research/state-of-the-creator-economy/)). Faster Hindi growth is claimed but unverified.
- **Both:** add the owner's Hindi voice-over as a second audio track; testers got 25%+ of watch time from other languages ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/youtubes-multi-language-audio-feature-for-dubbing-videos-rolls-out-to-all-creators/)).

## 6. Recommendation

**Space documentaries** on cosmic scale and danger, in English, owner-narrated, with a Hindi audio track: two 9–14-minute videos weekly (Sunday, Tuesday), 3–4 bridge Shorts, and a monthly compilation. Space sits in AIR's top-RPM category, has abundant free visuals, low policy risk, and a verified newcomer near 100K; the owner's voice and scripting are the cheapest defense against the inauthentic-content policy. With strong execution, a good 100-day outcome (my judgment) is YPP eligibility and 10–30K subscribers; 100K needs breakouts like Cosmic Lens's 2.5M-view [Pluto video](https://www.youtube.com/@CosmicLens-EN/videos).
