## Channel description (paste as is)

```text
The final moments of history's greatest tragedies, in under 30 seconds, and every claim sourced.

The Titanic's lookouts had no binoculars because the key to their locker left the ship in Southampton. Pompeii had felt earthquakes for years before Vesuvius buried it. History's Last Hours tells the true stories of shipwrecks, eruptions, lost cities, doomed expeditions, and the warnings nobody heeded, told through the people who lived them.

Our series:
- The Last Hours
- Lost Cities
- Doomed Expeditions
- Fallen Empires
- Warnings Ignored
- Sole Survivors

Every Short is researched from museums, archives, historians, and official inquiries, and its sources are listed in its description. Told with respect for the people who lived through these events; no gore.

Subscribe for a new story from history every day. For space, visit our sister channel, Universe Receipts.
```

## Channel trailer idea (about 30 seconds, vertical)

1. **Cold open (0 to 3 s):** Black screen, a ticking clock. Text: "April 14, 1912. 11:40 PM." Cut to the Stöwer
   Titanic painting.
2. **The hourglass (3 to 8 s):** The gold hourglass from the avatar fills the screen; its sand runs out.
3. **Rapid fire (8 to 24 s):** Three last moments, each on a period picture with a date card: Pompeii ("August 79 AD"),
   the Hindenburg ("May 6, 1937, 34 seconds"), Shackleton's Endurance ("November 21, 1915").
4. **The promise (24 to 28 s):** Voiceover: "The final moments of history's tragedies. Every claim sourced."
5. **Close (28 to 30 s):** "History's Last Hours. Subscribe."

## Voice of the channel

Gripping, grave, and exact. We tell each story like a narrator who knows how it ends and needs you to hear it, and
we never exaggerate beyond the sources. Every number has a source, estimates are called estimates, the dead are
treated with respect, and there is no gore. Only events at least 75 years old.
