M1 calls the rocky feature a slide and M2 calls it a crevice; a script should use the broader fact unless that detail is checked further. The Board concluded its escape fire “in no way impeded” the men seeking the ridge and said the evidence was inconclusive about how many understood Dodge’s directions. A relative had argued that the fire impeded escape; that statement is testimony, not the Board’s finding. The shared movement away from Dodge’s burned area must not be narrated as a simple, knowingly ignored rescue order.
