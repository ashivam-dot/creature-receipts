# History's Last Hours: lesser-known accident research slate

Drafted 2026-10-04. This is an **editorial research queue**, not a change to `CALENDAR.md`, an approved script, a release batch, or permission to host or schedule media. The scheduling hold remains in force.

## Editorial call

Make documented accidents a recurring part of **Warnings Ignored** and **The Last Hours**. The strongest story is a short, sourced chain: what someone saw, what they did, what happened next, and what the investigation could actually establish. “Hidden” and “world's most dangerous” are promises we cannot substantiate. Avoid rankings by body count. The calendar already has R101, Peshtigo, Port Chicago, Quintinshill, St. Francis Dam, and other accident leads; these ten are distinct from those entries.

No channel performance claim can guide this choice yet. The 2026-10-03 owned API snapshot in `analytics/2026-10-03.json` showed zero public videos and no usable Shorts-feed or retention sample after Winton was withdrawn. After the four corrected pilots in the current release plan, test two accident stories once their own editorial release gates are met. Compare only age-matched videos with actual feed exposure. Do not increase output or clear a hold on the strength of this slate.

## First three to develop

1. **Quebec Bridge** — a direct, original commission report supports the observed deformation and the commission's finding. The report drawings can make the failure understandable without disaster footage.
2. **Oppau explosion** — unusually strong official and technical sources support a surprising routine practice; the recent technical reassessment warns against oversimplifying the chemistry.
3. **Texas City disaster** — the contemporary investigation gives a minute-by-minute harbor sequence. It needs a careful image-rights review before production.

Each hook below is a *proposed opening angle*, not final narration. Every spoken and on-screen claim still needs claim-level citations from directly inspected sources and an independent review under `strategy/STRATEGY.md`.

| 2 | **Oppau explosion**, Germany; **21 Sep 1921** | “Workers had used small blasts to break up caked fertilizer thousands of times. | 3 | **Texas City disaster**, United States; **16–17 Apr 1947** | “A cargo fire was found aboard the *Grandcamp* in the morning. ## Source and production notes

- The Quebec Royal Commission OCR, Texas investigation, FFI report, ARIA record, Mann Gulch Board report OCR, Forest History Society history, TSHA entry, Museum Wales account, Donora Public Health Service OCR, Hong Kong Government notice, AJPH paper, Victoria County History, and Hansard pages were reachable and read on 2026-10-04. The Coast Guard *General Slocum* PDF returned HTTP 403 from this environment; NYPL's account was read. The Senghenydd inquiry scan loaded but lacks OCR, so its findings were not directly checked. The exact proposed hooks are constrained accordingly.
- Institutional hosting establishes a source's provenance, not an image license. For each chosen still, record the depicted subject, creator, date, collection item, license or permission, crop, and on-screen label. Do not borrow unrelated disaster footage for atmosphere.
- Develop **Quebec Bridge, Oppau, and Texas City** first. For any pilot, write a source map at the claim level, check competing findings and tolls, locate two suitable event-specific visuals, then decide whether the story fits the 22–35 second target. Give a complicated point more time rather than stripping away a material qualification.
- This queue deliberately does not enter `strategy/CALENDAR.md` or `status/stories.json`; the studio reads the calendar automatically, and the channel's release hold must remain unaffected.
