# 100-day plan

Day 0 is 2026-10-01 (the first two Shorts go out that day); Day 100 is 2027-01-09. Production runs around the
clock and keeps up to two weeks of finished Shorts ready; publishing follows `auto.PACE`: 2 a day from Day 0,
3 from Day 15, 4 from Day 30, and 5 from Day 50. Every step past 2 is earned by the last 10 Shorts keeping 90% of
the engaged views per Short and of the engaged share of the 10 before them, and a 30% fall in engaged views per
Short drops the pace back to 2 (`strategy/STRATEGY.md`, Publishing). That makes about 290 Shorts by Day 100 if
the pace holds at 3, and about 410 if every step triggers.

## What every Short looks like

Decided 2026-09-30 from the first renders and what holds viewers on Shorts: the first 1–3 seconds decide the
swipe, and a screen that moves keeps people longer than one that doesn't.

- **Moving footage wherever the real animal exists on film.** Each beat about the living animal (how it looks,
  moves, hunts, lives) gets HD or 4K footage of that exact species from Pexels and Pixabay, and the hook gets
  footage whenever there is some. The picker judges each clip by its frame, not its stock title, and the
  pre-render check and the reviewer both reject a similar-looking wrong species; a rejected clip falls back to a
  photo.
- **Receipts stay archival.** Discovery, history, and document beats keep the real period photos, museum
  specimens, and plates: they are what no stock-footage channel has, and the channel's name promises them.
- **Every still moves.** A depth model separates near from far, and the camera moves through the picture in
  2.5D instead of sliding over a flat print. Long beats cut to a close-up on the word being stressed.
- **Unchanged:** word-by-word captions, sound effects on cuts and reveals, the loop back to the first frame,
  two-site fact-checking, and the 4-out-of-5 quality gate.
- **Checked by numbers, not taste:** each Short's `review.json` lists where every beat's picture came from. The
  Day 21 review compares engaged share for Shorts that are mostly footage with Shorts that are mostly stills; if
  footage doesn't win, the picker stops preferring it.

## Targets

These are the targets we steer by. They aren't promises. Views are engaged views (what YPP counts; loops
excluded). Subscribers follow from views at the genre's measured conversion of about 0.9–1.3 per 1,000 views
(`research/channel/niche-market.md`, section 3); the base case assumes 1.2 and the stretch 1.25. Shorts counts are
the base pace (holding at 3) and, in brackets, the maximum.

| By | Base case | Stretch |
|---|---|---|
| Day 14 (Oct 15) | 30 Shorts, 30k views, 40 subscribers | 400k views, 500 subscribers |
| Day 30 (Oct 31) | 78 (79) Shorts, 180k views, 220 subscribers; 7-day median 1,000+ views a Short | 2.5M views, 3,100 subscribers |
| Day 50 (Nov 20) | 138 (160) Shorts, 1.2M views, 1,400 subscribers | 10M views, 12,500 subscribers, YPP applied |
| Day 75 (Dec 15) | 213 (285) Shorts, 5.2M views in 90 days, 6,200 subscribers | 25M views, 31,000 subscribers |
| Day 100 (Jan 9) | 288 (410) Shorts, 10M total views, about 12,000 subscribers | 40M+ views, 50,000 subscribers |

What the base case means for money: 10M engaged views in 90 days is reached around Day 100, after
mid-December, so in the base case YPP before the Feb 1, 2027 change needs either a breakout or an application
in January (review takes about a month, and YouTube hasn't said which bar applies to pending applications).
The **floor** is below the base: fewer than 50k total views by Day 30, which triggers the Day 30 format change.

## Decision points

- **Day 3 (Oct 4):** Read the first Shorts' `research.json` and `review.json`. If claims are thin, pictures
  show the wrong species, or scripts read like fact lists, fix the prompts, `strategy/SCRIPT-RULES.md`, or the
  trusted sites, not the thresholds.
- **Day 7 (Oct 8):** Retire hook styles whose engaged share (engaged views / views, the API's stand-in for
  Studio's "stayed to watch") sits well below the channel's median (under 80% of it on at least 5 Shorts).
  There is no official cutoff, so the comparison is always with our own Shorts.
- **Day 14 (Oct 15):** Pick a voice from the blind test plus the retention data. The pace goes to 3 on Day 15
  by itself once 20 Shorts have 48 hours of numbers.
- **Day 21 (Oct 22):** Rank series by engaged views per Short and subscribers per 1,000 views, and structures
  by engaged share. Keep the top 3–4 series at 70% of output; the rest continue at a trickle so they can be re-tested.
- **Day 30 (Oct 31):** If the 7-day median engaged views per Short is still under 1,000, change the format
  (on-screen hook text, first frame, faster pacing, a different voice) before adding volume, and hold the
  step-up by moving the Day 30 entry of `PACE` in `pipeline/src/ytc/auto.py` later. Otherwise the code goes to
  4 a day by itself while the last 10 Shorts keep 90% of the engaged views per Short and engaged share of the 10
  before, and every Short must still score 4+ at the quality gate.
- **Day 45 (Nov 15), stretch check:** If no Short has passed 100k views and total engaged views are under 2M,
  the stretch case is off for this 100 days. The weekly report says so plainly, and the channel steers by the
  base case and YPP. Start the first long-form compilation from the best series.
- **Day 50 (Nov 20):** 5 a day on the same test (also automatic).
- **Cadence ceiling:** 5 a day. More than that starts to look mass-produced, which the Partner Program's
  inauthentic-content rule rejects, YouTube gives no boost for volume, and a channel that went from 3 to 5 a
  day reported its views collapsing (`research/channel/growth-playbook-2026.md`, section 4). If engaged views per
  Short fall 30%, the code drops back to 2 a day by itself; don't override it.
- **Day 60 (Nov 30), YPP path:** Check engaged Shorts views in the last 90 days.
  - 4M or more: on pace for 10M by mid-December; keep going and apply the day Studio shows eligibility.
  - 1M to 4M: apply for the fan-funding tier as soon as it's reached (500 subscribers and 3M views in 90
    days; whether it locks in the old ads thresholds is unverified), and still apply for the ads tier the
    day it's reached, up to Jan 31.
  - Under 1M: the ads tier before Feb 1 is unlikely. Target the 20M bar, and weigh the format, the owner's
    own voice, and weekly long-form compilations, whose watch hours count toward the 4,000-hour path
    (`research/channel/growth-playbook-2026.md`, upgrade 7).
- **Day 60–75:** Apply for YPP as soon as Studio shows eligibility, aiming for mid-December (Day 75, Dec 15).
- **Any breakout** (3x the median of the channel's Shorts at the same age within 48 hours, or 100k+ views;
  `growth.BREAKOUT_*`): the studio queues follow-ups on the same animal or angle; the owner pins a comment and
  sets the Short's Related video to the next episode, within 48 hours.
- **Every Sunday:** weekly review in `reports/`, next week's experiments, and a check for better open-source tools.
- **Day 100 (Jan 9):** Review against both cases, and decide the next 100 days (including a regular long-form
  schedule).

## Owner touchpoints

- **Before launch:** create the accounts and sign in (START-HERE and `OWNER-CHECKLIST.md`). The agent never
  types passwords or codes.
- **Early:** phone verification and advanced features on the channel, needed for the Related video link,
  pinned comments, and the YPP application.
- **Optional:** TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook accounts for native cross-posting; recording narration in
  the owner's own voice if YouTube ever questions the channel.
- **When eligible:** the YPP application, AdSense, and the tax form (about 20 minutes).
- Nothing else is required. Daily reports land in `reports/`.
