Privacy policy for Creature Receipts tools

Effective 30 September 2026

Creature Receipts tools is a private program that the owner of the Creature Receipts YouTube channel runs on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses the Creature Receipts channel through two Google scopes: YouTube (youtube.force-ssl) and YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly). It reads the channel's public details and statistics, the list of its videos, and analytics reports such as views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers gained and lost, and traffic sources.

It changes only the channel's own content, in two ways: it creates the channel's series playlists and adds the channel's own Shorts to them, and it may publish or remove comments that YouTube has held for review on the channel's videos. It never uploads, edits, or deletes videos.

How the data is used

Only to measure how the channel's own videos perform, to decide what to make next, and to organize the channel's own videos into playlists.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token and the analytics reports are stored on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and not used for advertising.

Creature Receipts tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and deletes the stored reports when they are no longer needed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the data can be sent to the channel owner at aksha.shivam18@gmail.com, or through the Creature Receipts YouTube channel.
