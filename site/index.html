Creature Receipts

Animal stories. With receipts.

The peacock mantis shrimp throws a punch at about 51 miles per hour. The coelacanth, a fish scientists thought had died out with the dinosaurs, was found alive off South Africa in 1938. History's Last Hours tells the true, surprising stories of strange animals, extreme biology, and deep-sea life in under a minute. Every claim is backed by at least two reputable sources, listed in each Short's description.
