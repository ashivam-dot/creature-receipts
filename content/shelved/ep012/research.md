# ep012 research: First live footage of the colossal squid (Deep Sea Files)

Researched 2026-10-01 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemma-4-31b-it) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: For a century after its scientific description in 1925, the colossal squid was a ghost. Known mostly from dead specimens, beaks, and scars on sperm whales, the heaviest invertebrate on Earth had never been filmed alive in its natural habitat. That changed on March 9, 2025. During an expedition near the South Sandwich Islands in the South Atlantic, an international team using the Schmidt Ocean Institute's remotely operated vehicle SuBastian captured the first confirmed live footage of the species. Instead of a tentacle-thrashing monster, the camera revealed a translucent juvenile, measuring about 12 inches long, drifting at a depth of 600 meters. The sighting bridged a century-long gap in marine biology, giving scientists their first real look at the living animal in the deep sea.

## Disputed or left out

- Maximum length estimates vary between sources, with some citing total lengths up to 14 meters when measuring stretched tentacles on dead specimens while body length estimates generally cite up to 7 meters. Scripts should attribute maximum lengths to dead specimen measurements.
