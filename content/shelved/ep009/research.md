# ep009 research: The Smiling Salamander That Can Regrow Its Brain (Built Different)

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemma-4-31b-it) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: The axolotl is a real-life superhero of the animal kingdom. This smiling Mexican salamander possesses the astonishing ability to completely regrow severed limbs, its tail, and even parts of its heart and brain. Yet while millions thrive in labs and home aquariums worldwide, their wild ancestors face a heartbreaking crisis. Native only to the shrinking water channels of Lake Xochimilco in Mexico City, wild axolotls have suffered a staggering population crash due to urban expansion, pollution, and invasive fish like tilapia that eat their eggs. Surveys indicate that only about 50 to 1,000 adult axolotls remain in the wild, leaving this iconic, forever-young amphibian critically endangered. Conservationists are now fighting to restore their natural wetland habitats and testing whether captive-bred individuals can successfully make a comeback in the wild.
