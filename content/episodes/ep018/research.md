# ep018 research: The Truth Behind the 2025 Dire Wolf Pups (Back From Extinction)

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemma-4-31b-it) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In 2025, biotechnology company Colossal Biosciences announced they brought back the extinct dire wolf by creating three genetically modified pups named Romulus, Remus, and Khaleesi. Rather than cloning from tissue or injecting ancient DNA directly, scientists extracted endothelial progenitor cells from modern gray wolf blood samples. Using CRISPR technology, they made 20 edits across 14 genes to produce dire wolf traits like a pale coat, larger body size, wider head, and a powerful shoulder structure. The edited nuclei were placed into denucleated ova, and the resulting embryos were implanted into surrogate domestic dogs. Born via planned cesarean sections, the pups have exhibited wild wolf behaviors, such as howling from two weeks old and keeping their distance from humans. However, independent experts and the IUCN argue the animals are merely hybrid gray wolves rather than true de-extinct dire wolves, pointing out that dire wolves diverged millions of years ago.
