Story: In September 1944, British Field Marshal Bernard Montgomery launched Operation Market Garden, a bold plan to end World War II before Christmas. The strategy relied on dropping over 30,000 Allied paratroopers into the Netherlands to capture key bridges along a narrow corridor, allowing armored ground forces to cross the Rhine into Germany. Despite intelligence warnings that elite German SS panzer divisions were refitting near the objective, Allied commanders pressed forward. Major General Roy Urquhart's British 1st Airborne Division was dropped up to eight miles away from their main target, the road bridge at Arnhem. As bad weather delayed reinforcements and radio communications broke down, Lieutenant Colonel John Frost's small force reached the bridge but was cut off. Facing heavy armor and overwhelming German attacks, the isolated paratroopers held out for days before being overwhelmed, leading to the failure of the operation.
