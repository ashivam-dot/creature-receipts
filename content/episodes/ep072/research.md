# ep072 research: Why Did a Routine Fertilizer Blast Destroy Oppau? 💥 (The Last Hours)

Researched 2026-10-05 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: On the morning of September 21, 1921, workers at the BASF chemical plant in Oppau, Germany, used small explosive charges to break up thousands of tons of caked ammonium sulfate nitrate fertilizer stored in a massive silo. Having performed this routine procedure safely thousands of times before, they trusted the mixture was non-hazardous. Without warning, the stockpile detonated with unimaginable force, creating a crater 90 meters wide and 20 meters deep. The blast obliterated eighty percent of Oppau, shattered windows thirty kilometers away in Heidelberg, and was heard over 275 kilometers distant. More than five hundred people lost their lives, and nearly two thousand were injured. Investigations later revealed that a recent spray-drying modification had inadvertently rendered the porous fertilizer highly explosive, transforming a standard industrial technique into a catastrophic disaster.
