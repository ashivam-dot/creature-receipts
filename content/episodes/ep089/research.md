# ep089 research: Why the Last Emperor of China Became a Gardener 🌸 (Fallen Empires)

Researched 2026-10-06 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemma-4-31b-it) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Puyi's life was a tragic sequence of falls from heights few can imagine. Crowned at age two, he was treated as a living god in the Forbidden City, where his every sadistic whim was indulged while he remained a virtual prisoner of ancient etiquette. In 1912, he was forced to abdicate, ending 2,000 years of imperial rule and beginning a decades-long descent into political irrelevance. He spent his adulthood as a pawn, eventually serving as the puppet emperor of Japanese-occupied Manchukuo. After the war, he was captured by Soviet troops and later imprisoned by the Chinese Communist Party for 'reeducation.' In a final irony, the man who once owned all of China was released in 1959 to live as a humble citizen. He spent his remaining years tending plants as a gardener at the Beijing Botanical Gardens, near the very palaces where he had once been worshipped as the Son of Heaven.

## Disputed or left out

- Post-release occupation: Source S6 mentions Puyi worked as a street sweeper, while S1 and S3 specify he was a gardener and handyman at the Botanical Gardens.
- Repatriation date: Source S1 states Puyi was repatriated to China in 1950, while S6 states it occurred in 1949.
- His attitude toward his later life: S3 describes him as solemn and perhaps regretful, while S6 emphasizes his epiphany and kindness in his final years.
