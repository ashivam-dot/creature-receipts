# ep065 research: The Bend Measured Before Quebec Bridge Fell (Warnings Ignored)

Researched 2026-10-04 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: On August 27, 1907, the bend in lower chord 9-L of the unfinished Quebec Bridge was found to have greatly increased. Bridge staff measured deflections and discussed their danger. On August 29, the bridge collapsed over the St. Lawrence River. The Royal Commission found that defective lower-chord design caused the collapse. It did not believe action after August 27 could have prevented the fall, but said better judgment by those responsible might have prevented the loss of life.

## Disputed or left out

- The origin of the bends in the lower chords: inspectors on site argued that the bends occurred after erection due to stress from the growing weight, while Phoenix Bridge officials and designer Birks argued the bends had been present in the chord before shipping or were made in the shop. The script should explain that while workers on-site recognized the danger of buckling under weight, corporate management tried to dismiss it as a pre-existing manufacturing flaw.
